Role Summary

Affiliate & BD Consultant - Cync Digital

Audited and redesigned partner-program SOPs across 7 engagements, scaling the managed partner base to 140+ (60+ active, revenue-generating). Redesigned Day 1/3/7 onboarding SOPs in HubSpot automation, restructured commission tiers into a Tiered CPA/RevShare/Hybrid structure, and built a compliance-checked payout review process that improved overall program ROI. In parallel, running ongoing market and partner intelligence across fintech, SaaS, e-commerce, digital-services, and hosting categories - tracking competitor partner programs, campaign mechanics, and monetization structures to surface market-expansion and activation opportunities. Separately designed a complete partner/affiliate pipeline build system for partner acquisition across 5 programs: recruitment funnel, tiered commission structures, onboarding workflow, and tracking/attribution setup. The system is fully designed and ready to operationalize; shortlisting is underway through relevant channels and communities.

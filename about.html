About Cync Digital

A small studio with a direct line to the person doing the work.

Cync Digital is an independent partnerships and performance growth practice based in Dhaka, Bangladesh and working remotely with brands worldwide.

Cync Digital is led by Naimur Rahman Ferdous, who brings over three years of hands-on experience building and managing performance-based partner networks from zero: negotiating commission structures, owning go-to-market execution, and running partner and affiliate programs across SaaS, fintech, IT services and e-commerce.

That track record includes building an affiliate program from scratch to 100+ partners across creator and publisher channels, and managing a 140+ partner base (60+ active and revenue-generating) through the Cync Digital practice.

The studio is deliberately small. That is a design choice: clients get senior attention on every engagement, decisions are made quickly, and nothing is lost between an account manager and an executor. Where a program needs more capacity than one person can responsibly provide, it is said during scoping and not discovered mid-project.

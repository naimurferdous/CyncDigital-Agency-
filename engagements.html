FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Is Cync Digital an agency or a freelancer?+

It is a founder-led boutique studio. There is no account layer and no junior handoff: the person who scopes the work does the work. If a program needs more capacity than one person can deliver, I say so during scoping.

What does it cost?+

Every engagement is scoped to program size and goals, so there is no rate card. After a short discovery call you receive a written proposal with scope, deliverables, timeline and fee. Fees can be fixed, monthly or performance-linked.

How do we start?+

Send a brief through the contact form. I reply within one business day, we hold a 30 minute discovery call, and you receive a proposal if there is a fit.

Do you sign NDAs?+

Yes. An NDA can be signed before any program data is shared, and client names stay confidential where an NDA applies.

How is performance reported?+

Reporting follows the format shown on the Work page: EPC, conversion rate, cohort benchmarks and partner-level detail. Cadence (weekly or monthly) is agreed in the proposal.

Which platforms do you work in?+

NetRefer (admin), HubSpot, GA4, AppsFlyer, SEMrush, Notion and Google Workspace day to day, and familiarity with Impact, Awin and Everflow.

Can you guarantee results?+

No honest partner marketer can. I guarantee the process, the transparency of reporting, and a prompt response when performance drops. Outcomes depend on offer, market and budget.

Who owns the assets, SOPs and dashboards?+

You do. Trackers, SOPs, reporting templates and documentation are handed over at the end of the engagement.

Where are you based and what hours do you keep?+

Dhaka, Bangladesh (GMT+6), working remotely with overlap across EU, US and APAC business hours.

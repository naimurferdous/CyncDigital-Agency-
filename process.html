How I Work

A clear process, start to finish.

Four phases - so you always know what stage you're in and what happens next.

Phase 01

Audit & Foundation

I pull existing partner and performance data, segment what's actually working, and map every commission model against compliance. You can't scale what hasn't been measured first.

Phase 02

Strategy & Build

I design the commission structure, activation sequence, and recruitment targeting - then build the tracking and automation to run it.

Phase 03

Launch & Activate

I recruit with intent - targeting the partners most likely to convert, not the biggest follower counts - and run the onboarding sequence that gets them active fast.

Phase 04

Optimize & Report

I A/B test, tighten what's underperforming, and hand over a full results report against baseline - with the tracker and SOPs so the program keeps running smoothly.

Every engagement is scoped to your program's size and goals. Tell me where you're starting from on the Contact page and I'll give you a straight answer on fit and cost before we go further.
